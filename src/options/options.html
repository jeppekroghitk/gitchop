Access to GitHub

General Menu GitHub

Panels

What rises when you press the dot: the links and the search, the pull requests column, the news column, and the contributions count. The first three have pages of their own under Menu.

Links

The links the menu shows, top to bottom.

Any of these in a link’s URL is filled in from the page you are on. A link that cannot be filled in is greyed out in the menu.

The chop

How the page is chopped open when you press the dot. Changes save on their own; Preview plays them here.

Tokens

A token unlocks private repository search, the pull requests, news from private repositories, your contributions, and backing your links up to a secret gist.

Index

GitHub’s search does not return private repositories, so gitchop keeps its own list of the repositories your token can reach, on this machine only, and matches that first.

Backup

Your links live in this browser’s profile and go with the extension if it is removed. Backup writes them to a secret gist on every change.

Pull requests

A column beside the menu: feedback on your pull requests, the ones waiting on your review, and yours waiting on others.

News

A column on the far side of the menu with what happened in the repositories you follow, a few sentences each. It is made up once a day at the hour set here and covers everything since the previous edition.
